How It Works

1

Face Detection

YuNet deep-learning face detector validates that a prominent human face is present. Non-face images are rejected.

2

Feature Extraction

58 handcrafted features extracted — 12 colour (RGB + HSV), 20 GLCM texture, 26 LBP histogram bins.

3

ML Classification

7 traditional ML models trained (SVM, RF, KNN, DT, LR, NB, XGBoost). Best model selected by 5-fold CV accuracy.
